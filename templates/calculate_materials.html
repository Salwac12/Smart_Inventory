How Are Quantities Calculated?

Required Quantity: Quantity per PCB × Production Quantity

Order Quantity: Maximum of (Required Quantity − Available Stock, 0)

Estimated Purchase Cost: Order Quantity × Inventory Unit Price

If several BOM rows use the same inventory component, the system combines their requirements before calculating shortages.

Important: These calculations do not deduct or reserve stock. They also do not account for material wastage, other production orders, incoming purchases, or supplier minimum order quantities.
