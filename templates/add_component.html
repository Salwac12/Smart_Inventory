What Happens After Saving?

The new component will be registered in your inventory database.

1. The component becomes available in your Inventory Management page.

2. You can perform Stock IN and Stock OUT operations.

3. The component can be selected during BOM Inventory Matching.

4. Its stock quantity can be used in Material Requirement Calculations.
