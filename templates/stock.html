Understanding Stock Operations

📥 Stock IN

Use Stock IN when components are received from suppliers, returned to stores, or added through a verified stock adjustment.

📤 Stock OUT

Use Stock OUT when components are issued for production, testing, consumption, or a verified stock adjustment.
